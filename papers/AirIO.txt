G. Ablation Study on Model Compression

   To evaluate the compressibility of different representations,
we introduced additional two lightweight models Light A and
Light B. The light models keep the same layer structure but
shrink the dimensions of each layer's hidden units. Finally, the
encoder's latent feature dimension is reduced from 256 to 128,
and then further to 64, yielding progressively smaller models.

   To quantify the performance degradation as the model is
compressed, we define the degradation ratio for ATE and
RTE. A higher degradation ratio indicates a larger drop in
performance. As shown in VII, the model under body frame
shows smoother degradation in both ATE and RTE.
